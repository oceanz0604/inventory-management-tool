1 Logging in

Opening the app shows the login screen. Signing in has three quick steps:

Company codeThen tap Continue
UsernameYour name
PasswordThen Sign In
Stay logged in: Once you sign in, the app remembers you. Next time it opens straight to your screen — no need to log in again unless you sign out.

2 Who can do what (roles)

Every login has a role. The role decides which screens and buttons are visible.

Owner
Full access — products, stock, billing, orders, reports, khata, customers, and managing the team.
Office
Day-to-day management: products, stock, orders, purchases, customers/sellers, khata, reports. Cannot manage the team.
Staff
Sees only the POS counter for fast billing at the shop.
Marketing
Sees only Field Orders — to collect orders from shops while out in the market.

3 Getting started wizard

The first time an owner logs in, a short setup wizard appears and walks you through the basics:

  1. Welcome — a quick introduction.
  2. Locations — add your shop / godown / outlets.
  3. Products — add the items you sell or make (choose a type — see section 6).
  4. Inventory — add opening stock as a purchase (with cost and, if needed, an expiry date).
  5. Finish — you're ready!
You can pause anytime: The wizard remembers where you stopped. Close the app and come back later — it resumes from the same step. Add as many items as you like on each step, or Skip and add things later from the menu.

4 Dashboard (your home screen)

The home screen is your daily health check. At the top is a welcome bar with quick action buttons (e.g. New Sale, Add Product). Below are tappable cards and charts:

  • Number cards — Products, Stock Value (₹), Sales Today (₹), Pending Orders, Units in Stock, Low Stock. Tap any card to jump to that screen.
  • Revenue · last 7 days — a chart of your recent sales.
  • Stock by location, Top products by value, and a Recent activity list.

5 Locations

Locations are your physical places — a shop, a warehouse, an outlet. Stock is tracked separately for each location.

  • Tap Add Location, give it a name (e.g. "Main Shop"), and save.
  • You can have as many locations as you need.

6 Product types & adding products

Products now come in three types. Choosing the right type keeps your costs and selling accurate.

Raw materialOnly used as an ingredient. Never sold or published. No selling price — its cost comes from each purchase batch.
Simple (trading)You buy it and resell it as-is. Has a selling price. Buy cost comes from your purchase batches.
Complex (made)You make it from a recipe of ingredients. Has a selling price; the making cost is calculated when you produce it.
  1. Open Products → Add Product and pick the type.
  2. Enter the name, unit, and (for simple/complex) a selling price.
  3. For a complex product, add its recipe: choose each ingredient and how much is used. The making cost shows automatically.
  4. Save. (Raw & simple products get their cost from the stock batches you add later.)
Searchable dropdowns: Every dropdown (category, product, customer, seller…) has a search box. Just start typing to find an item quickly — very useful once you have hundreds of products.

7 Inventory: batches & lots

Stock is tracked as batches (lots). Each batch remembers how many, the cost per unit, an optional expiry date, and the supplier. This is more accurate because you often buy the same item at different prices at different times.

  1. On Inventory, tap a product row to expand and see its batches at each location.
  2. Tap Add stock / batch, enter quantity, unit cost, (optional) batch number, expiry and supplier.
  3. Set a minimum stock level so the app warns you when it runs low.
Automatic & smart: When you sell or fulfil an order, the app uses the batch that expires first (FEFO) so nothing goes to waste — and it records the real cost of what you sold. You can also pick a specific batch by hand.

8 Produce (making complex items)

Manufacturing now lives inside Products — there is no separate "Recipes" page. To make a complex product:

  1. Open Products → tap Produce.
  2. Choose the complex product, the location, and the quantity to make.
  3. Pick which ingredient batches/lots to use (or let the app choose the oldest first).
  4. Confirm. The ingredients are consumed, a new finished batch is created, and its making cost is calculated from the exact lots used.

9 POS counter (billing)

The POS (Point of Sale) is for fast counter billing — the main screen for Staff. Only sellable products (simple & complex) appear here.

  1. Choose the location, then tap products to add them to the bill (tap again to increase quantity).
  2. If an item has several batches, you can pick which lot to sell from.
  3. Choose payment and tap Complete Sale.

Each completed sale reduces the right batch's stock and is recorded for reports.

Manual purchase

When you buy stock from a seller who is not on the marketplace, use New Manual Purchase:

  1. Pick the seller (a custom Seller you added — see section 11).
  2. Add the products, quantities, prices, and (optional) expiry per item.
  3. Save. When you receive the order, a new stock batch is created automatically with that cost.

11 Customers / Sellers

Here you keep your own list of Customers and Sellers who are not on the platform — just a name and location. This lets you:

  • Record purchases from sellers who don't use the app.
  • Choose a customer when a marketing person places a field order.

12 Field orders (marketing)

A marketing person roams the market and collects orders from shops. Instead of carrying paper back, they place the order directly in the app:

  1. Select the Customer (or add a new one on the spot with Add customer).
  2. Add the products and quantities the shop ordered (all published items show, even if stock is short — it's an internal order).
  3. Submit. The order instantly reaches the office.

At the office, the Office or Owner sees it, accepts and delivers. If stock is short they can cancel it. If payment isn't received, it goes into the Khata as credit and the order stays open.

13 Khata (credit ledger)

The Khata is your digital udhaar (credit) book. It shows who owes you money and how much. Unpaid orders appear here automatically. Record payments as customers clear their balance.

14 Shop (marketplace)

The Shop is a B2B marketplace to browse products from other suppliers on the platform and add them to a cart for purchase orders. For sellers who aren't on the platform, use a Manual Purchase (section 10) instead.

15 Reports

Reports turn your data into charts and summaries — sales trends, top products, stock value, expiring batches and more — so you can make better decisions.

16 Team management

Owners can add team members and choose their role. Open Team → Add member:

  1. Enter the member's name and a username.
  2. Choose a role: Office, Staff, or Marketing.
  3. Set a password and save. They log in with your company code + their username + password.
Usernames are unique within your company. Two members can't share the same username.

17 Tips, theme & install

  • Dark / light theme: tap the moon/sun icon to switch.
  • Bottom bar shortcuts (owners): choose which buttons appear on the mobile bottom bar from More → Customize bottom bar — e.g. a manufacturer can keep Orders and Field instead of POS.
  • Install on your phone: open the site in your browser and use "Add to Home Screen" to use it like an app, even offline.
  • Sign out: use the menu to sign out when you're done on a shared device.

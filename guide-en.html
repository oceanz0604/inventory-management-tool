Complete User Guide

1 Logging in

Opening the app shows the login screen. Signing in has three quick steps:

  1. Enter your Company Code (given to you when your account was created) and tap Continue.
  2. Type your Username.
  3. Type your Password and tap Sign In.
Stay logged in: Once you sign in, the app remembers you. The next time you open it, it goes straight to your screen — no need to log in again unless you sign out.

2 Who can do what (roles)

Every login has a role. The role decides which screens and buttons are visible.

Owner
Full access — everything below: products, stock, billing, orders, reports, khata, customers, and managing the team.
Office
Day-to-day management: products, stock, orders, purchases, customers/sellers, khata, reports. Cannot manage the team.
Staff
Sees only the POS counter for fast billing at the shop.
Marketing
Sees only Field Orders — to collect orders from shops while out in the market.

3 Getting started wizard

The first time an owner logs in, a short setup wizard appears. It helps you add your basics so the app is ready to use:

  1. Welcome — a quick introduction.
  2. Locations — add your shop / godown / outlets.
  3. Products — add the items you sell or make.
  4. Inventory — set the opening stock for each product.
  5. Recipes — (optional) if you manufacture, define what goes into a finished item.
  6. Finish — you're ready!
You can pause anytime: The wizard remembers where you stopped. Close the app and come back later — it resumes from the same step. You can add as many items as you like on each step, or Skip a step and add things later from the menu.

4 Dashboard

The home screen gives you a quick health check of your business: total products, current stock value, recent sales, low-stock alerts and charts. Use it as your daily starting point.

5 Locations

Locations are your physical places — a shop, a warehouse, an outlet. Stock is tracked separately for each location.

  • Tap Add Location, give it a name (e.g. "Main Shop"), and save.
  • You can have as many locations as you need.

6 Products & Categories

Products are the items you buy, make or sell. Categories help you group them (e.g. "Snacks", "Drinks").

  1. Open Categories and add a few groups (optional but handy).
  2. Open Products → Add Product. Enter name, category, selling price, and unit.
  3. Save. The product now appears in inventory, POS and orders.

7 Inventory (stock)

The inventory screen is a grid of products × locations. Each cell is how much of that product you have at that location.

  • Tap a cell to set or adjust the quantity.
  • Stock goes down automatically when you sell at the POS or fulfil an order, and up when you receive a purchase.

8 POS counter (billing)

The POS (Point of Sale) is for fast counter billing. This is the main screen for Staff.

  1. Tap products to add them to the bill (tap again to increase quantity).
  2. Review the items and total.
  3. Choose payment and tap Complete Sale.

Each completed sale reduces stock and is recorded for reports.

9 Orders & purchases

The Orders screen tracks both sales orders (going out) and purchase orders (coming in).

Manual purchase

When you buy stock from a seller who is not on the marketplace, use New Manual Purchase:

  1. Pick the seller (a custom Seller you added — see section 10).
  2. Add the products and quantities you bought, with prices.
  3. Save. When you receive the order, stock increases automatically.

Accept, fulfil & settle

Incoming orders can be accepted, fulfilled (delivered), and settled (paid). If an order is delivered but not paid, it is automatically added to the Khata as credit and stays open until cleared.

10 Customers / Sellers

Here you keep your own list of Customers and Sellers who are not on the platform — just a name and location. This lets you:

  • Record purchases from sellers who don't use the app.
  • Choose a customer when a marketing person places a field order.

11 Field orders (marketing)

A marketing person roams the market and collects orders from shops. Instead of bringing paper back to the office, they place the order directly in the app:

  1. Select the Customer (or add a new one on the spot with Add customer).
  2. Add the products and quantities the shop ordered.
  3. Submit. The order instantly reaches the office.

Back at the office, the Office or Owner sees the order, accepts it, and delivers. If stock is short, the office can cancel it. If payment isn't received, it goes into the Khata as credit and the order stays open.

12 Khata (credit ledger)

The Khata is your digital udhaar (credit) book. It shows who owes you money and how much. Unpaid orders appear here automatically. Record payments as customers clear their balance.

13 Recipes / BOM

If you manufacture goods, a recipe (Bill of Materials) defines which raw materials make one finished product. This helps track what's consumed when you produce items.

14 Shop (marketplace)

The Shop is a B2B marketplace to browse products from other suppliers on the platform and add them to a cart for purchase orders. For sellers who aren't on the platform, use a Manual Purchase (section 9) instead.

15 Reports

Reports turn your data into charts and summaries — sales trends, top products, stock value and more — so you can make better decisions.

16 Team management

Owners can add team members and choose their role. Open Team → Add member:

  1. Enter the member's name and a username.
  2. Choose a role: Office, Staff, or Marketing.
  3. Set a password and save. They log in with your company code + their username + password.
Usernames are unique within your company. Two members can't share the same username.

17 Tips, theme & install

  • Dark / light theme: tap the moon/sun icon to switch.
  • Bottom bar shortcuts (owners): you can choose which 4 buttons appear on the mobile bottom bar from More → Customize bottom bar — e.g. a manufacturer can keep Orders and Field instead of POS.
  • Install on your phone: open the site in your browser and use "Add to Home Screen" to use it like an app, even offline.
  • Sign out: use the menu to sign out when you're done on a shared device.
